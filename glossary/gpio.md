# GPIO

**GPIO** stands for **General Purpose Input/Output**.\
These are general-purpose pins on a microcontroller that can be configured as **input** or **output**, allowing interaction with sensors, buttons, LEDs, relays, and other devices.

#### Main Functions

* **Digital input** → reads states: HIGH/LOW (1 or 0).
* **Digital output** → controls devices: turns LEDs, relays, buzzers on/off.
* **Special functions** (depending on the chip):
  * PWM (brightness/speed control)
  * ADC (analog reading)
  * I2C, SPI, UART
  * Internal pull-up/pull-down resistors





***

#### One-sentence summary

GPIO is the basic interface that enables the microcontroller to read information from the external world and control physical devices.
