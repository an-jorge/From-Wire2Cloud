# CLOCK

### Clock

A **clock** is a timing signal that defines **when and how fast** a digital system operates.\
It synchronizes the execution of instructions and coordinates the operation of the microcontroller’s internal components. &#x20;

#### Main Functions

* Defines the **CPU execution speed** (clock frequency).
* Synchronizes internal peripherals and buses.
* Controls timing for communication protocols (UART, SPI, I2C).
* Affects **power consumption** and overall system performance.



***

#### One-sentence summary

The clock is the timing reference that synchronizes all operations inside a microcontroller, defining its speed, timing accuracy, and power consumption.
